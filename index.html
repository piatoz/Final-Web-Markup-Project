Section 1: Meet the XM5

Quick overview of the XM5

The XM5 is a mirrorless camera, which means it has no internal mirror. Light goes directly onto the sensor at all times, so the flip-out screen shows a live preview of your final image before you press the shutter. If you change the aperture, shutter speed, ISO, or film simulation, you see the effect on screen right away, so you can fix a photo that's too dark or too bright before you take it.

Main controls

The three controls you'll use mainly are the mode dial (switches between auto, manual, video, film recipes, and more), the film simulation dial (changes the look of your photos instantly), and the command dials (adjust settings like aperture and shutter speed).

Section 2: Lenses

Prime Lenses

A lens with a single, fixed focal length (like 23mm or 35mm) that cannot zoom in or out. It offers sharper image quality, lighter weight, and wider maximum apertures for low-light shooting and background blur, and it keeps the XM5 pocket-sized.

Zoom Lenses

A versatile lens with a variable focal length range (like the 15-45mm kit lens) that lets you zoom in and out with a twist of the ring. It's convenient for travel and vlogging since you can change your framing instantly without swapping lenses.

What kind of lens does the XM5 need?

The X mount: The XM5 uses Fujifilm's X mount, so you need lenses made for it. Lenses for other systems (Canon, Sony, Nikon) won't fit directly. Fujifilm makes two lens lines for the X mount:

  • XF Lenses

    The premium line, with metal builds and wide apertures.

  • XC lenses

    The budget-friendly line, lighter and more compact.

    • Both lenses fit the XM5, so you can pick based on budget and size.
